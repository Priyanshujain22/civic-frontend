How It Works

1. Report

Submit your complaint with detailed descriptions, exact GPS locations, and high-quality photo evidence.

2. Assign

Our automated system routes your issue directly to the concerned government officer or verified local vendor.

3. Resolve

Track real-time progress right from your dashboard. We'll notify you the moment the issue is fixed!

About the System

Bridging the gap between active citizens and effective municipal administration.

Our goal is to provide a fully transparent, hyper-efficient platform for citizens to report critical civic issues. From road damages to broken streetlights, authorities can now seamlessly manage, instantly route, and securely resolve community complaints in real-time.

  • Real-time resolution GPS tracking
  • 100% Transparency in administration
  • Direct communication channel to vendors
